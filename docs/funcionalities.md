# Funcionalities OBD-C

## User functionalities

---

### Sharing

- Associate a car with a user account
- Invite members to a group to share the car data with them
- Show the split of the costs of the car between the members of the group. Primarily focused on fuel, maybe could then add maintenance or insurance (fuel, maintenance, etc.)
- Calendar indicating when each member of the group is using the car, so that they can plan their trips accordingly.
- QR code to allow other users to scan and join the group, or to allow them to use the car for a specific period of time (e.g., in case of trip, the other users are able to easily split the count of the fuel)

### Data

#### Historical data

> Maybe not so useful, but could be interesting to record this data in case of selling the car or in some other cases. Nice to look at but not really an important functionality.

- Total kilometers driven (to check if possible)
- Maximum speed reached
- Average speed (not sure if it makes sense)
- Total hours driven

#### Wrapped data

> Should be more useful to the user, as it can help them understand their driving habits and fuel consumption. Preferably, this data should be displayed in a graph or chart to make it easier to understand. Time period coule be configurable by the user, but a default period of one month could be set.

- Total kilometers driven
- Total hours driven
- Total liters of fuel consumed
- Most visited destination

#### Real-time data

- Percentage of fuel/liters of fuel
- Show if there is any problem with the car
- Tire pressure (probably not possible)

### Location

- Most visited place
- Trip history
- Geofencing
- Anti-theft system
- Save the location of the car when it is parked, so that the user can find it later

### Notifications

- Notify the user when the car is being used by someone else (if the car is shared)
- Notify the user when the car is being used outside of a geofenced area (if geofencing is enabled)
- Notify the user relevant data of the car, such as low fuel, maintenance needed, etc.

## B2B

Primarily focused on car rental companies, but could also be useful for car dealerships or other businesses that have a fleet of cars.

---

### Dynamic pricing

Based on the data collected from the use of the car, the insurance company or car rental company could offer dynamic pricing for the insurance or rental of the car. For example, if the user is driving in a dangerous way, the insurance company could increase the price of the insurance, or if the user is driving in a safe way, the insurance company could decrease the price of the insurance.

### Historical data

- Total kilometers driven (to check if possible)
- Maximum speed reached
- Average speed (not sure if it makes sense)
- Total hours driven

### Location

- Geofencing
- Trip history

### Notifications

- Notify the client when the car is being used outside of a geofenced area (if geofencing is enabled)
- Notify the client when the user is driving in a dangerous way (e.g., speeding, harsh braking, etc.)
- Notify the client if the user broke a rule of the road (e.g., speeding in school zone, etc.)
